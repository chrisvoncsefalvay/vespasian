## Design Context

### Users
Vespasian serves people experimenting with model behaviour through direct weight editing. Users configure an objective, examples, scoring rules and a frontier inference budget, then inspect each candidate and save the retained checkpoint. The Hugging Face workbench supports newcomers through guidance and experienced users through detailed measurements.

### Brand Personality
Calm, precise and professional. The creator asks for complete, direct language, an exceptional interface and clear evidence beside each editing decision.

### Aesthetic Direction
Preserve the existing charcoal, cream and copper palette, typography and restrained editorial layout. Give the objective chart visual priority during a run. Use readable controls and deliberate spacing across desktop and mobile.

### Design Principles
- Keep setup, observation and completed results focused on their current task.
- Connect decisions to examples, measurements and the affected model layers.
- Introduce features through optional guidance that users can dismiss and reopen.
- Keep language concrete and use complete sentences.
- Use soft, brief motion to explain transitions and acknowledge interactions.

### Motion and Performance
The workbench runs inside Gradio and Hugging Face, including on mobile. Run output can refresh twice a second. Animate only deliberate navigation, the guide and meaningful state changes, without replaying entrance effects on routine refreshes. Prefer opacity and transform, keep feedback below 300ms, preserve immediate focus and input, and disable motion when the user requests reduced motion. Add no animation dependency or continuous rendering loop.
