02 · The method

The willclimb.

It's a hill climb... for weight space.

The editor reflects on generated answers, proposes a direct tensor alteration and reads the result. And you get to see it all.

Explore the search loop ↗
  1. 01

    Set a direction

    Define the behaviour, connect the examples and choose the metric – including what must be preserved.

  2. 02

    Do weight surgery

    The editor selects tensors, an operation, a magnitude and a seed. Vespasian applies this bounded change to the model.

  3. 03

    See the result

    Vespasian generates rollouts and compares objective scores. Then, it runs basic capability preservation checks alongside them.

  4. 04

    Keep what works

    Yeet what doesn't. At the end, export the patch that prevailed.

03 · Your data, your objective

Start with what
you want to change.

Load a local JSONL or a Hugging Face dataset. Preview the records, map the fields and set how many examples to use.

examples/concise.jsonl ↗ PAIRED · SAMPLE 
{"prompt":"What is the capital of France? Give only the answer.","target":"Paris","split":"edit"}

Choose exact match, token overlap or goal scoring. Score each generated answer against its reference.

$ vespasian examples/concise.jsonl --goal "Answer precisely" --budget 2
